Synology says customers should update devices immediately after a rise in C3RB3R ransomware activity against NAS appliances. The report suggests targeted opportunistic attacks on exposed storage systems, but it does not specify exploit details in the summary.

Why it matters: Active ransomware targeting of a widely deployed storage platform creates immediate defender concern, especially for organizations using NAS for shared data and backups. The report indicates an ongoing attack wave rather than a theoretical risk.

Action: Patch Synology devices promptly, verify external exposure, and ensure offline backups and recovery procedures are tested.
