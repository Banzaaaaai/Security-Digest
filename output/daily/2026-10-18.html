Compromised ConnectWise credential used to access Microsoft 365 tenants

US 50% · Europe 30% · MEA 10% · Other 10% · Global IT services 90% · Energy services 40% · Identity and privileged access 95% · SIEM, monitoring and detection engineering 70%

Attackers are abusing a compromised ConnectWise Cloud Backup credential to sign in to Microsoft 365 tenants. The campaign targets customer environments through a trusted MSP-related account path.

Why it matters: This is an active credential-abuse campaign affecting a widely deployed enterprise platform and a managed service provider supply-chain path. It has direct implications for Microsoft 365 tenant security and MSP trust relationships.

Action: Audit third-party and MSP access paths into Microsoft 365, enforce strong conditional access and MFA protections, and monitor for anomalous sign-ins across customer tenants.

Detection assessment: No source-linked detection rules were provided. Detection should focus on anomalous M365 sign-ins, risky OAuth/session activity, and MSP credential abuse patterns.
