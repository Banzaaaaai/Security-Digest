CRITICAL · Priority 97.25

Breach at Belfast-based supplier affects dozens of Irish credit unions and went unnoticed for six weeks

US 10% · Europe 100% · MEA 5% · Other 5% · Retail banking 95% · Global IT services 60% · Data protection and DLP 85% · Audit readiness, governance and risk 80%

Ireland's Data Protection Commission says it has received more than 40 breach notifications from credit unions. They used a Belfast-based company as a data processor, and that company suffered a breach. The breach reportedly went undetected for about six weeks.

Why it matters: A third-party breach has affected many regulated retail financial institutions in Europe. That fits the rubric's 'major breach in a target industry in Europe'.

Action: Review the security controls and breach-notification SLAs of data processors. Check which third parties hold member data. Prepare for possible phishing aimed at affected members.

Detection assessment: Assessment: the six weeks without detection suggests the supplier's monitoring was weak. Customers should review the visibility and breach-notification terms they have with their suppliers.

Sources: The Record

Amazon: suspected China-linked actor used AI agents to exploit MONAI Label zero-day for root access

US 45% · Europe 45% · MEA 40% · Other 40% · Government 30% · Global IT services 25% · Vulnerability and exposure management 70% · AI-assisted work and AI governance 65%

Amazon Threat Intelligence reports that suspected China-linked attackers used autonomous AI agents to discover an unauthenticated path-traversal file-write flaw in the open-source MONAI Label medical imaging server and gained root on a university server. MONAI Label 0.8.5 and earlier are affected in the default configuration. Attribution is described as suspected.

Why it matters: Confirmed in-the-wild exploitation of an unauthenticated flaw, but MONAI Label is niche medical/research software, not widely deployed enterprise technology. AI-agent-driven discovery is a notable TTP shift.

Action: Inventory MONAI Label instances, remove internet exposure, apply a fixed version when available, and review hosts for unexpected files or root-level activity.

Detection assessment: No published detection rules in sources; assess web logs for traversal sequences in upload requests and unexpected file writes outside MONAI storage directories.
